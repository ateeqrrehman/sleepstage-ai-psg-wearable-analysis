# Data Card

## Dataset

PhysioNet Sleep-Accel Dataset

## Data Sources

- PSG sleep-stage labels
- Heart rate
- Wrist acceleration
- Step counts

## Label Structure

Sleep stages are represented as 30-second epochs.

## Data Considerations

- Wearable signals may contain noise and missing intervals.
- Subject-level variability may impact generalization.
- Sleep-stage imbalance can affect classifier behavior.

## Privacy and Ethics

The dataset is publicly released for research purposes through PhysioNet.
